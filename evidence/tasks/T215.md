## Implementation

`partitions.py` sorts finite inputs by normalized UTC instant and source-row number, rejects duplicate identities, and keeps equal instants intact. It counts `[t0,t0+24h)` as warm-up and chooses the whole-group development boundary nearest the 3,000,000 source-row default, including warm-up, with earlier ties. Scoreable development rows receive nearest 70%/85% cumulative boundaries and nonempty train/validation/test partitions. Integer-scaled distances keep tie decisions exact. The result contains ordered IDs, counts and UTC boundary instants; extra mapping keys, including labels, are ignored.

The helper materializes rows and IDs. It is for synthetic finite batches. A measured external sort, bounded publication pass and real-source temporal counts remain separate gates; this task does not publish T210 partitions or authorize an aggregate label audit.
