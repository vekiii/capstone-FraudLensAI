## Scope and proposed T215 boundary

Rows carry stable `source_row_number`, opaque `transaction_id` and timestamp text. The result exposes ordered ID tuples and reconciling counts for warm-up, all scoreable rows, development/showcase source rows, and train/validation/test scoreable development rows. A small `development_target` overrides the production 3,000,000 source-row target solely for hand-computed fixtures; the production default remains FR-016.

Cases cover `[t0,t0+24h)` and exact-boundary eligibility, including two offset-equivalent spellings at one instant; deterministic chronological order from shuffled input; inclusion of warm-up in the development source count; a development boundary equidistant between whole groups choosing the earlier group; a closer later whole-group choice; independent 70% and 85% scoreable-development boundaries with earlier exact-distance ties; no label-driven changes; and controlled failure when nonempty train/validation/test groups are impossible. Each case keeps equal-instant rows together.
