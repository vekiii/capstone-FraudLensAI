## Races and lifecycle

Every selection, retry, back action and disposal increments a generation *before*
aborting the prior request. Detail content is replaced synchronously with loading.
Only the current generation may render success/error or clear the active request.
Back restores the selector and focus to its case button. Disposal aborts pending
work and prevents late results from repopulating a removed view. HMR invokes cleanup.

Four added UI lifecycle tests independently inject a client that ignores abort,
so generation guards are tested separately from T017's transport cancellation.
They check deterministic order/selected state/back focus, late success, late
failure and cancellation/disposal. No original T013 assertions were changed.
