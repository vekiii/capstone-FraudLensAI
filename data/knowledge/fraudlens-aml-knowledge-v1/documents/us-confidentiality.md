# US affiliate confidentiality

## Scope
Historical FinCEN FIN-2010-G006, scope paragraph and confidentiality discussion. Short exact excerpt: "This guidance does not address the applicability of any other Federal or state laws." Authored educational summary: the guidance concerns sharing within specified US affiliate relationships and preserving confidentiality. This is not blanket permission to disclose reports, a current-law assessment or a Serbian rule. No report or private client content is reproduced.
