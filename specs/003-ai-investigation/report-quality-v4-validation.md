## Access boundaries and custody

A trusted OFFLINE evaluator/custodian (actual identity/automation version recorded
before execution; none invented here) owns frozen requirements and PRIVATE expected
calibration judgments. Runtime generator/tools/indexes never receive expected
ratings, held-out references, judge outputs or gold canaries. Before generation
freeze/hash model/settings/prompts/code/public case inputs/evidence plus separate
private references and role assignments. Save one-shot markers/all outputs/errors/
usage/unknown outcomes. A new live batch needs its own explicit budget authorization.

Judges see predeclared source-derived essential-fact obligations, a fixed disclosure
policy, source evidence/report, and a NEUTRAL inventory of contract field names.
The inventory is identical across every report and calibration (transaction_id,
amount,currency,event_time,score_meaning,source_of_funds,identity,occupation,device,
history,human_authority). It says NOTHING about which fields are missing, invented,
incorrect or expected to pass. Source values/statuses come only from authorized
source evidence, not evaluator conclusions. Essential-fact obligations are fixed
from case inputs BEFORE report generation, never derived from expected judge flags.
There is no evaluator-derived unknown/missing-field list in a judge input.

The packet projector MUST DROP legacy reference.expected_missing_fields, expected
judgments, reviewer notes and gold canaries entirely; it substitutes the same fixed
assessment_fields inventory and policy for all packets. This is distinct from
reports/sources themselves saying a value is unknown, which is the evidence the
judge must evaluate. These assessment requirements are NOT outcome labels. Judges do NOT see
gold calibration flags, peer ratings/reasons or generator labels. Explicit packet
projection keeps gold-only canaries outside; packet_id hashes bind exact content,
roles and versions. No expected-label file may enter generation or judge prompts.

## Explicit report pass equation (final review correction, not yet re-reviewed)

A report passes ONLY when ALL of the following are true:
- generation and rating are valid and present;
- factual denominator >0 and support_rate >=0.90;
- essential denominator >0 and essential_coverage >=0.80;
- required_disclosures_complete is true;
- missing_values_not_invented is true;
- no_cross_section_contradictions is true;
- missingness_correct, computed as the AND of those three predicates, is true;
- every procedure unit is applicable (unsupported OR uncertain fails the report);
- additional_issues is empty, including any unbound/unsupported guidance elsewhere;
- report and unit critical flags are all false;
- no unresolved unit or predicate disagreement exists.

The three predicate gates are mandatory independently of numerical support/coverage.
Every recommended check is represented by a procedure unit; compound prose is split
into atomic units, and recommendations outside enumerated units must be recorded
as additional_issues. This prevents ungraded recommendations from disappearing.
Empty denominators are unavailable, never a perfect score. Pack acceptance also
requires successful exact6/6 calibration, eight retained primary slots, >=7 passing
reports, zero critical errors across all eight, and the fixed paired-five reviews.

Mandatory T096 negative boundary tests: C2 (all numeric gates good but missing
human-authority disclosure) fails; one unsupported/uncertain procedure fails;
unsupported guidance outside enumerated units fails via additional_issues;
C3/C6 fail despite a correct unknown-data disclaimer; invalid calibration leaves
quality_pass and passing_reports unavailable even if all raw ratings look good.
